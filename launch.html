Terrace Dashboard

The Terrace Dashboard is a private tool used only by Terrace Construction, Inc. It runs on the owner's own computer and reads QuickBooks Online for one company. It is not offered to anyone else.

To open or reconnect it, start the dashboard on that computer and go to http://127.0.0.1:3000/admin.html, then choose "Connect QuickBooks (Intuit)".
